# 📊 Retail Business Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Analytics project built using **SQL (MySQL), Power BI, DAX, and Excel** to analyze online retail sales data (2010–2011). The project focuses on identifying sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and providing data-driven strategic business recommendations.

# 📌 Key Business Insights

- United Kingdom generates the highest revenue.
- Revenue peaks during the September–November period.
- Premium customers contribute the largest share of revenue.
- A small number of products drive a significant portion of total revenue.
- Heavy dependence on a single market (UK) increases business risk.

---

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

- Reduce dependency on the UK market by expanding into additional high-potential countries.
- Strengthen retention of Premium customers through loyalty programs and personalized offers.
- Increase inventory and marketing efforts before the September–November peak season.
- Prioritize top-performing products in inventory planning and promotional campaigns.
- Implement customer retention strategies for At Risk and Inactive customers.
